Privacy Policy

Task Check

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Task Check. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Task Check handles information when you use our application.

2. Information We Collect

Task Check does not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on any external server. The app does not require account registration or login and does not use a backend server to provide its core functionality.

Information you enter into the app, such as tasks and related details, is stored locally on your device.

3. Local Data Storage

Task Check uses local device storage to save your tasks and preferences so that they remain available when you reopen the app. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us through the app.

Uninstalling the application or clearing its local app data may permanently delete information saved by the app. We cannot recover locally stored information if it is deleted.

4. Data Sharing

Task Check does not sell, rent, or share your locally stored task information with us or third parties. The app does not send this information to a backend server.

5. Third-Party Services

Task Check does not intentionally use third-party analytics or advertising services to collect personal information. However, your device's operating system or app distribution platform may independently process technical information under its own privacy practices.

6. Data Security

Since your task information is stored locally, its security depends partly on your device's security settings and access controls. We recommend keeping your device secure and using appropriate screen-lock protection.

7. Children's Privacy

Task Check does not knowingly collect personal information from children because the app does not collect or transmit personal information through its core functionality.

8. Your Control Over Your Data

You can manage your tasks directly within the app. You may delete individual tasks using the available app features. Clearing the app's local data or uninstalling it may remove all locally stored information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's functionality or privacy practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
